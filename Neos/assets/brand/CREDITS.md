# Logo sources

- `accio.svg`: vector export of the paper's `logo/accio.pdf`; original logo and colors preserved.
- `github.svg`: vector export of the paper's `logo/github-logo.pdf`; original logo and colors preserved.
- `huggingface.svg`: [Hugging Face official brand assets](https://huggingface.co/brand), downloaded from [hf-logo.svg](https://huggingface.co/datasets/huggingface/brand-assets/resolve/main/hf-logo.svg).
- `arxiv.svg`: [Simple Icons arXiv glyph](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/arxiv.svg), displayed in `#b31b1b`.

All files are served locally. Logos identify the corresponding project resources; the buttons remain disabled until actual release links are supplied.
